Workflow

From Idea to Completion

Streamlined project lifecycle powered by AI intelligence

Project Registration

Students and faculty register on the platform, submit their interests, and their information is securely stored for project allocation.

AI Student–Faculty Matching

The AI engine intelligently matches students with the most suitable faculty mentors based on research interests, domains, and expertise.

Project Idea Submission

Teams submit their project title, abstract, objectives, and keywords for initial proposal submission.

AI Originality Check

Advanced AI analyzes the project for originality, semantic similarity, plagiarism detection, and provides intelligent improvement suggestions before review.

Research Papers

The AI recommends relevant research papers and literature based on the submitted project domain to help teams improve their project quality.

Faculty & Coordinator Review

Faculty evaluates the project using AI-powered insights and recommendations. The project coordinator provides the final approval before development begins.

Development & Weekly Progress Tracking

Faculty assigns weekly tasks while teams collaborate, submit progress reports, receive feedback, track milestones, and communicate through the integrated project management dashboard.
